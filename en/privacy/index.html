Privacy Policy — Medipath.AI

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Portuguese source

Introduction

Medipath.AI values privacy, security and responsible use of personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be processed when using medipath-ai.com, the Medipath.AI platform and related features, including public pages and forms made available by healthcare professionals through Presença Online.

Personal data is processed in accordance with applicable law, including Brazil's General Personal Data Protection Law (LGPD).

Who Medipath.AI is

Medipath.AI is a technology platform intended to assist healthcare professionals and teams in organizing care, procedures, patient information, documents and related workflows.

The Medipath.AI platform is operated by Medipath Tecnologia Ltda. — CNPJ: 68.777.766/0001-10.

For matters concerning privacy, data protection or use of the platform: Email: contact@medipath-ai.com.

Roles in data processing

When physicians, clinics or healthcare professionals use Medipath.AI to organize information concerning their patients and care, those professionals or organizations are normally responsible for care-related decisions.

Medipath.AI provides the platform and processes care-related data under applicable professional instructions and the law. Medipath remains responsible for its own duties as the platform provider, including appropriate security and access controls; these duties are not transferred entirely to the physician or clinic.

Medipath.AI may also act as the party responsible for processing certain data of its own, such as account, security, support and service-operation information.

Data processed

The platform may process data concerning professionals, users and patients, including registration information, contact details, care-related data, clinical information, questionnaires, documents, uploaded files and records necessary for platform operation and security.

Health information is considered sensitive personal data under the LGPD and receives protection appropriate to its nature.

Presença Online

Presença Online allows professionals to make public pages available to facilitate initial contact and receipt of patient information.

Patients may submit registration information, answer questionnaires and attach documents related to care. This information is forwarded to the Medipath.AI environment corresponding to the responsible professional.

Submitting a request does not guarantee confirmation or provision of care.

Purposes of processing

Data may be used to operate the platform, authenticate users, organize patients and procedures, receive requests, store documents, enable communication between professionals and authorized teams, maintain security, provide support, comply with legal obligations and improve the service.

Medipath does not sell patient data.

Information security

Medipath.AI adopts technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or improper processing.

These measures include user authentication, access control, logical separation between professionals' environments, protection of files against unauthorized public access, secure connections, controlled credential management, and backup and recovery procedures.

Storage and infrastructure

The main data of the Medipath.AI platform is stored in infrastructure located in Brazil, including services configured in the São Paulo region.

The infrastructure includes database and object-storage services configured to meet requirements for security, availability and protection of information processed by the platform.

Backup and recovery

Medipath.AI maintains scheduled backup procedures covering the application database and files held in platform object storage.

Backups are kept in infrastructure separate from the main application, also located in Brazil, enabling recovery procedures in the event of technical incidents.

Data subjects' rights

Under the LGPD, data subjects may request information about processing of their data, including access, correction, information on sharing, deletion where applicable, and other rights provided by law.

Requests may be sent to contact@medipath-ai.com.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting Medipath or the professional responsible for your care through the channels in this Policy. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing or retention required by law.

This Policy does not limit rights provided by other mandatorily applicable data-protection laws. Where applicable, data subjects may also petition Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

Patient registration and purpose

Where available on the professional’s page, Presença Online allows patients to register, including people outside Brazil. The information requested and collected depends on the form and workflow used and may include name, date of birth, country, identification document and issuing country, contact details, health information, questionnaire answers and uploaded files. Government identification for international patients is optional in workflows that support that option; an identification document, passport, date of birth and contact details are not required from every patient. CPF requirements remain in place in Brazilian workflows where they apply. This data helps the professional identify the patient, assess the request and organize care. Provide only necessary information.

Responsibilities and data protection

The physician or clinic generally determines the lawful purpose and basis for processing care-related data, informs the patient and obtains consent where legally required. Medipath provides the platform and processes that data under applicable instructions, as well as for its own purposes already described in this Policy. Patient consent or professional authorization does not replace each party’s other legal obligations. Access controls and separation between professionals’ environments are maintained to restrict access to authorized people.

Storage and support services

The platform’s main records are stored in Brazil. Hosting, storage and operational support providers process data to deliver the service. Depending on the service or feature used, including AI features subject to the conditions below, these providers may process information outside Brazil.

Supabase provides database, authentication and storage services; Amazon Web Services provides backup infrastructure; Replit hosts the application; and Resend delivers transactional account emails. OpenAI participates in processing when the professional uses AI features, under the conditions described below.

The data shared depends on the service used: registration and authentication data for account access and email delivery; patient information, documents and files to operate the platform; copies of records for service recovery; and information relevant to the request, text or audio when the professional uses AI. The access controls and separation between environments described in this Policy continue to apply.

Temporary request processing does not rule out retention of security or monitoring logs. For text and live voice, OpenAI documents default retention of those logs for up to 30 days, with legal or security exceptions. This does not establish Zero Data Retention or absence of storage outside Brazil.

OpenAI data processing and retention rules

Conditional use of artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence features are used under the professional’s control and are subject to applicable permissions. When these features are used, information relevant to the request, text or audio may be processed by OpenAI to assist with organizing information or transcription. Their use must comply with applicable legal requirements, including those for health data; the professional’s permission does not replace those requirements. AI-generated results require professional review. Submitting the registration form does not automatically trigger AI or, by itself, establish a legal basis for that processing.

Retention, rights and contact

Data retention takes account of processing purposes and legal and professional obligations, including retention of clinical records. Deleting active records does not immediately remove every backup copy. Some backups currently have no automatic deletion period defined. Deletion requests are assessed in accordance with applicable law and retention obligations. Under applicable law, you may request information, access, correction and deletion where applicable, and exercise the other rights described in this Policy. Contact the professional responsible for care or Medipath at contact@medipath-ai.com. Registration is not consent to a medical procedure and does not guarantee care.

Changes to this Policy

Medipath.AI may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the platform, infrastructure, security procedures or applicable law.
