Introduction

Medipath.AI values privacy, security and responsible use of personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be processed when using medipath-ai.com, the Medipath.AI platform and related features, including public pages and forms made available by healthcare professionals through Presença Online.

Personal data is processed in accordance with applicable law, including Brazil's General Personal Data Protection Law (LGPD).

Who Medipath.AI is

Medipath.AI is a technology platform intended to assist healthcare professionals and teams in organizing care, procedures, patient information, documents and related workflows.

The Medipath.AI platform is operated by Medipath Tecnologia Ltda. — CNPJ: 68.777.766/0001-10.

For matters concerning privacy, data protection or use of the platform: Email: contact@medipath-ai.com.

Roles in data processing

When physicians, clinics or healthcare professionals use Medipath.AI to organize information concerning their patients and care, those professionals or organizations are normally responsible for care-related decisions.

Medipath.AI provides the technology infrastructure necessary to process that information, applying the security and processing controls needed to operate the platform.

Medipath.AI may also act as the party responsible for processing certain data of its own, such as account, security, support and service-operation information.

Data processed

The platform may process data concerning professionals, users and patients, including registration information, contact details, care-related data, clinical information, questionnaires, documents, uploaded files and records necessary for platform operation and security.

Health information is considered sensitive personal data under the LGPD and receives protection appropriate to its nature.

Presença Online

Presença Online allows professionals to make public pages available to facilitate initial contact and receipt of patient information.

Patients may submit registration information, answer questionnaires and attach documents related to care. This information is forwarded to the Medipath.AI environment corresponding to the responsible professional.

Submitting a request does not guarantee confirmation or provision of care.

Purposes of processing

Data may be used to operate the platform, authenticate users, organize patients and procedures, receive requests, store documents, enable communication between professionals and authorized teams, maintain security, provide support, comply with legal obligations and improve the service.

Information security

Medipath.AI adopts technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or improper processing.

These measures include user authentication, access control, logical separation between professionals' environments, protection of files against unauthorized public access, secure connections, controlled credential management, and backup and recovery procedures.

Storage and infrastructure

The main data of the Medipath.AI platform is stored in infrastructure located in Brazil, including services configured in the São Paulo region.

The infrastructure includes database and object-storage services configured to meet requirements for security, availability and protection of information processed by the platform.

Backup and recovery

Medipath.AI maintains scheduled backup procedures covering the application database and files held in platform object storage.

Backups are kept in infrastructure separate from the main application, also located in Brazil, enabling recovery procedures in the event of technical incidents.

Data subjects' rights

Under the LGPD, data subjects may request information about processing of their data, including access, correction, information on sharing, deletion where applicable, and other rights provided by law.

Requests may be sent to contact@medipath-ai.com.

Changes to this Policy

Medipath.AI may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the platform, infrastructure, security procedures or applicable law.
